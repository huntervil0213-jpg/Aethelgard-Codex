# 16 — MAGIC, SPELLCRAFT & CASTING

## Purpose

This file tracks the deep history of magic in Aethelgard, how different peoples cast it, and how ancient practices changed across ages.

## 1. The First Current

Long before kingdoms, the world was threaded by a natural magical current later called Flux. Ancient peoples did not initially treat it as a fuel source. It was understood as part of the living world: moving through roots, stone, weather, blood, beasts, and the spaces between planes.

The oldest traditions describe three broad ways of interacting with magic:

- **Resonance** — magic generated or projected through a living body.
- **Conduit craft** — magic directed through an external focus, crystal, weapon, staff, rune, or engineered channel.
- **World-binding** — magic persuaded through places, spirits, roots, elements, celestial forces, or ancient agreements.

These categories predate most modern magical schools.

## 2. Casting Types Across the Ages

### Body Casting
The caster becomes the focus. No wand, staff, or artificial conduit is required. Abel's Resonance is an unusually powerful example.

### Blood Casting
Magic is inherited through a bloodline. Blood-casters often possess abilities unavailable to ordinary practitioners, but inherited power can also become politically controlled.

### Focus Casting
A physical object carries or shapes magical force. Staffs, rods, wands, crystal matrices, weapons, rings, and engineered Ley-Tech devices can serve as focuses.

### Rune Casting
Magic is prepared through symbols, inscriptions, geometric patterns, or carved arrays. Ancient rune systems can remain active long after the original caster dies.

### Pact Casting
Power is accessed through an agreement with a powerful entity, spirit, deity, primordial force, or other supernatural intelligence.

### Ritual Casting
Several participants combine time, preparation, components, and coordinated magical intent. Rituals are slower but can accomplish effects beyond ordinary combat casting.

### Environmental Casting
A caster draws from a location rather than solely from their own reserves. Ancient groves, ley intersections, elemental regions, ruins, and the Ancestral Root can dramatically change what is possible.

### Resonant Casting
The caster synchronizes with an existing magical frequency. This is particularly important to Ley-Tech and advanced elven disciplines.

### Sympathetic Casting
An object connected to the target acts as an anchor. Hair, blood, possessions, names, crafted effigies, or personal relics can be used depending on the tradition.

### Echo Casting
A magical action is recorded into an object, location, or living medium and triggered later. Some ancient civilizations used this for defensive arrays and long-term wards.

## 3. The Ancient Schools

The oldest surviving classifications include Frost, Flame, Storm, Root, Stone, Tide, Shadow, Radiance, Void, Spirit, Blood, Bone, Time, Dream, and Resonance. These are not identical to modern spell schools.

Modern scholars often classify magic by effect. Ancient cultures frequently classified it by relationship: what the caster was asking the world to do and what the world demanded in return.

## 4. Frost / Resonance

Frost is more than creating ice. At advanced levels it can alter heat exchange, slow magical motion, interfere with energy flow, and crystallize unstable magical currents.

Abel's frost is unusual because it manifests through his body rather than a conventional artificial focus. His inability to control it reliably is therefore both a combat weakness and evidence of his abnormal Resonance.

Queen Althaea recognizes that his family misunderstood the phenomenon by treating his body as a battery rather than a living magical conduit.

## 5. Ley-Tech

Ley-Tech is the technological discipline of directing natural magical currents through engineered systems. It can power infrastructure, weapons, barriers, transport systems, archives, and stabilization arrays.

Its greatest danger is scale. A machine that safely redirects a small current can become catastrophic when connected to an entire region's ley network.

The Spire's industrial extraction systems represent one of the most destructive applications of Ley-Tech.

## 6. Monolith Stabilization

The Monolith Stabilization Arrays were developed to regulate large-scale magical instability. Kael-Vossen preserves knowledge associated with these systems.

The arrays do not simply generate power. Their primary function is to keep existing magical systems from becoming unstable enough to damage reality itself.

## 7. Reality Fissures

Reality fissures are not ordinary portals. They are structural failures between layers of existence. Prolonged magical extraction, damaged ley networks, planar pressure, ancient weapons, or unknown forces may contribute to their formation.

The violet tear appearing beyond Kael-Vossen represents an existential threat because continued widening can destabilize regions far from its point of origin.

## 8. Ancient Spellcraft

The oldest spells were often not spoken commands. Some were songs, gestures, dances, blood markings, geometric arrangements, names, contracts, or acts of deliberate sacrifice.

Spoken incantations became increasingly common because language gives a caster a repeatable mental structure. Different cultures consequently developed radically different spell traditions.

## 9. Spell Components

A spell may use one or more of the following:

- verbal structure
- somatic movement
- material component
- magical focus
- blood or life force
- environmental energy
- rune or prepared array
- pact authority
- inherited Resonance

Not every tradition requires all of them.

## 10. Ancient Magical Ages

### The First Age — Before Written Kingdoms
Magic was inseparable from nature. Dragons, elementals, fey, giants, and early mortal peoples interacted with the world's currents without standardized spell theory.

### The Age of First Names
Civilizations began recording magical phenomena. Early scripts attempted to categorize forces that had previously been understood only through oral tradition.

### The Age of Concord
Multiple peoples exchanged magical knowledge. Early forms of the True Alliance emerged from this tradition of shared resources and technological development.

### The Age of Extraction
Political powers began treating ley energy as an economic resource. Magical engineering shifted from cooperation toward industrial control.

### The Age of the Spires
Vertical empires expanded their use of monoliths, Ley-Tech, filtration systems, and artificial conduits. Knowledge was centralized and historical records were increasingly controlled.

### The Fracturing Age
Large-scale extraction and accumulated magical damage contributed to increasingly severe instability. Modern reality fissures are among the most visible consequences.

## 11. Spell Design Rules for the Novel

New spells should have a reason to exist, a cultural origin, a recognizable casting method, and a cost or limitation appropriate to their power.

Ancient magic should not automatically be stronger than modern magic. It should be different, less standardized, and sometimes harder to understand.

Magic should affect characters emotionally and physically where appropriate rather than functioning as consequence-free spectacle.
