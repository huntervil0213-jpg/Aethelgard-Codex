## Terms from Character Speech

### Spark
A particularly useful example of Aethelgard's cultural vocabulary. A human can be called a Spark without it being childish or demeaning. In one character's mouth it may mean admiration for human adaptability; in another's it may be bitter commentary about humanity's tendency to ignite wars.

### Thrum's language
Thrum can use forge and stone metaphors as affectionate insults. His dwarven profanity should remain distinct from the speech patterns of the other outcasts.

### Elara's language
Elara's street background allows sharper, practical insults and locally understood expressions. Her religious references can invoke Solis naturally when emotion spikes.

### Kaelen's language
Kaelen can use direct martial terminology, especially when danger is immediate. His speech should remain grounded rather than turning every statement into a threat.

### Abel's language
Abel generally avoids extravagant profanity, but extreme emotional pressure can break that restraint. His royal vocabulary should occasionally surface despite his rejection of the title.

## Expansion Policy

Every future term should be tagged with:

- speaker/community
- region
- historical period
- literal meaning
- social meaning
- emotional tone
- whether it is respectful, neutral, affectionate, mocking, or hateful
- whether it is canon or optional
