# Kharzun Usage Guide

Kharzun is the story-specific Dwarvish register used in Aethelgard. It is not a transcription of real-world Dwarvish and must remain distinct from Common.

## Core rules
- Use Kharzun when dwarven identity, clan custom, engineering culture, oaths, or strong emotion is relevant.
- Thrum may insert short Kharzun phrases into Common dialogue naturally.
- Full Kharzun should appear in moments where dwarven speech would plausibly dominate: clan oaths, forge work, curses, arguments between dwarves, technical instructions, and ritual language.
- Keep translations deterministic: canonical vocabulary beats improvised vocabulary.
- Do not make every Kharzun word sound alike. Compounds should combine established roots without flattening the lexicon.
- Avoid accidental Common/English words when a Kharzun equivalent exists.

## Register
**Forge register:** technical, clipped, metaphor-heavy.
**Clan register:** formal, oath-heavy, ancestral.
**Street register:** blunt, abbreviated, insulting.
**Battle register:** imperative-heavy and extremely concise.

## Thrum
Thrum's speech should remain recognizable as Thrum even when Kharzun appears. His profanity is dwarven rather than elven, and his engineering metaphors are frequent but not constant.

## Translator compatibility
All canonical Kharzun entries are ASCII-safe. The external translator should reject unknown words rather than silently inventing translations.
