# Old Words

## Spark

Spark is an old name for humanity. Humans were not the first people of Aethelgard. The name came from the ancient belief that humans carried a strange capacity to kindle change, rebuilding themselves and the places around them again and again. Over centuries, the phrase Spark of Life became shortened to Spark.

Among elves, dwarves, and other older peoples, the word has never carried one single feeling. It can be affectionate, admiring, teasing, or bitter depending on the speaker and the history between them.

## Skull-Veil

Skull-Veil is the feared name given to the elven Inquisitors associated with brutal hunts and the enforcement of ancient elven authority. It is not another name for elves as a whole.

In regions touched by their hunts, the name is often spoken quietly. Some families use it to frighten children away from forbidden roads. Others refuse to speak it at all.

## Canopy House

Canopy Houses were old elven institutions built around hereditary stewardship, scholarship, territory, and living architecture. Their archives could contain generations of treaties, maps, genealogies, and magical records.

## High-Court Speech

The formal elven register used in sovereign courts, treaties, judgments, and diplomatic encounters. It carries social meaning beyond the words themselves.

## Forge-Born

An old dwarven honorific for a person whose life is closely bound to craft, stone, metal, or engineering.

## Iron-Marrow

A dwarven expression for stubborn endurance. Spoken warmly, it praises someone who refuses to break. Spoken bitterly, it means a person is too stubborn to change.

## Softpaw

A Feline-Kin expression for someone inexperienced with dangerous travel or life beyond protected settlements. Friends may use it affectionately; strangers may use it as an insult.

## Nine-Luck

An old Feline-Kin expression for someone who survives circumstances that should have killed them.

## Blood Right

The inherited political status claimed by certain royal dynasties. To lose blood right can mean losing legal identity, inheritance, title, and recognized place within a ruling family.

## Silt-Sinks

The name given to degraded industrial regions where extraction, waste, and environmental collapse have transformed once-livable land.

## Iron-Vein

The vast underground mineral and filtration networks that became central to industrial development in several kingdoms.

## Flux-Damned

A frontier curse born from generations of living around unstable magic and industrial Flux. It can express anger, disbelief, fear, or frustration.

## Void Take You

A severe curse invoking whatever exists beyond ordinary reality. It is most often heard when people speak about reality fissures, forbidden magic, or things they cannot explain.

## By Solis

An invocation associated with Solis. It can be reverent, casual, sarcastic, frightened, or desperate.

## The Spark of Life

An ancient phrase associated with humanity's reputation for renewal. It predates many modern human kingdoms and survives in poetry, old songs, and the speech of other peoples.

## The Living Root

An old elven phrase referring to the belief that a community's history remains connected to the land beneath it.

## Stone Memory

A dwarven idea that worked stone preserves the hands, decisions, and intentions of those who shaped it.

## Road-Luck

A traveler expression found in several cultures. It refers to the hope that whatever waits beyond the next bend is kinder than whatever was left behind.
