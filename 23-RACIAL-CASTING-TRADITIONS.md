# 23 — RACIAL CASTING TRADITIONS

## Dragons

True dragons cast through innate magical anatomy, breath, blood, memory, and elemental affinity. Their magic is not simply a spellbook tradition.

## Dragonborn

Dragonborn inherited fragments of ancient draconic magical principles but developed mortal casting traditions. Sorcerous bloodlines, martial techniques, clan rites, and learned spellcasting coexist.

## Elves

Elven magic traditionally emphasizes resonance with living systems, precision, ritual, memory, and environmental awareness. High Elven court magic tends toward controlled formal structures.

## Dwarves

Dwarven spellcraft favors runes, crafted focuses, metal, stone, engineering, and repeatability. A dwarven artificer may approach a spell like an engineer approaches a machine.

## Humans

Human magic is exceptionally diverse because humans adopted techniques from many older cultures. Wizardry, clerical traditions, sorcery, pact magic, bardic magic, and martial spellcraft all became widespread.

## Feline-Kin

Feline-Kin traditions commonly emphasize mobility, sensory enhancement, illusion, stealth, and environmental magic, though individual cultures differ greatly.

## Orcs

Many ancient orc traditions treat magic as communal rather than purely academic. Battle chants, ancestral rites, spiritual practices, and shamanic casting remain important in some cultures.

## Gnomes

Gnomish spellcraft often combines illusion, invention, alchemy, enchantment, and experimental magic.

## Halflings

Halfling traditions frequently emphasize protection, luck, community wards, hearth magic, agriculture, and travel magic.

## Tieflings

Tiefling magic varies according to ancestry. Infernal pacts, sorcery, inherited planar traits, and learned arcane traditions all occur.

## Aasimar

Aasimar magic can involve divine gifts, radiant power, celestial ancestry, healing, protection, and revelation.

## Genasi

Genasi often display elemental magic as an extension of their bodies rather than a purely learned discipline.

## Giants

Giant spellcraft traditionally connects magic to weather, elemental force, stone, fire, frost, and monumental ritual structures.

## Fey

Fey magic frequently operates through names, bargains, emotion, seasons, stories, glamour, and place. Its rules can appear irrational to modern arcane scholars while remaining internally consistent.

## Undead

Undead magic depends on origin. Necromancy, curses, soul-binding, divine punishment, parasitic magic, and residual magical trauma can all produce undead states.

## Aberrations

Aberrant magic may originate outside normal material-plane magical systems. Their abilities should not automatically obey mortal spell theory.

## Rule for Aethelgard

Race does not determine class. A Dragonborn can be a wizard, a dwarf can be a cleric, an elf can be a barbarian, and a human can possess an ancient bloodline. Culture influences available traditions; it does not dictate identity.
