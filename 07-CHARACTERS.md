# CHARACTERS

This is the main character reference for the novel. Keep the useful stuff here: names, race, appearance, personality, relationships, abilities, history, important events, and anything else that matters while writing.

## ABEL THORNE VALERIUS

- Race: Human
- Role: Former prince of the High-Reach; defector and member of the outcast group
- Magic: Frost-based Resonance
- Family: House Valerius
- Father: King Cassian Valerius
- Important history: His Resonance manifested at seven winters old. His family used him in alchemical experiments and forced his power through Ley-Tech conduits. He was eventually stripped of his bloodright and permanently banished.
- Personality: Controlled, protective, stubborn, burdened by guilt, and increasingly angry at what his family has done.
- Current goal: Help repair the damage caused by the Spire and stop the spreading reality fractures.

## ELARA SOL-VOSS

- Race: Human
- Role: Priestess and member of the outcast group
- Faith: Solis
- Personality: Street-smart, protective, blunt, emotionally intense beneath a hardened exterior
- Relationship: Closely bonded with Abel; her concern for him becomes especially intense when his bloodline and Resonance put him in danger.

## THRUM IRON-MARROW

- Race: Dwarf
- Role: Engineer and member of the outcast group
- Gear: Heavy forge wrench
- Personality: Grumpy, curious, practical, sarcastic, and easily distracted by interesting machinery

## KAE﻿LEN VANE-SKAR

- Race: Dragonborn-like / dragonborn-descended warrior
- Role: Tracker and frontline fighter
- Gear: War-hammer
- Appearance: Scaled, massive build, amber eyes
- Personality: Protective, suspicious, blunt, and quick to anger when the group is threatened

## VAELIN KAEL-THAR

- Race: Elf
- Role: Shadow rogue and scout
- Personality: Quiet, analytical, dry sense of humor, observant
- Skills: Stealth, scouting, reading threats and patrol patterns

## LYRA EMERALD-WEFT

- Race: Elf
- Role: Scholar and translator
- Appearance: Silver-white hair, emerald eyes
- Background: Member of a fallen canopy house. Her family's private archives contained old regional lexicons and boundary treaties.
- Language: Can recognize and translate old/high-court Elven but is rusty speaking it because she learned it through study rather than regular conversation.

## KIKIASIE

- Race: Feline-Kin
- Role: Rogue-like member of the outcast group
- Personality: Playful, sarcastic, perceptive, and protective when things become serious
- Gear: Heavy black-scaled book/record containing important coordinates

## KING CASSIAN VALERIUS

- Race: Human
- Role: King of the High-Reach
- Relationship to Abel: Father
- Notes: Represents the ruling power and exploitation of the Spire.

## QUEEN ALTHAEA

- Race: Elf
- Role: Queen of Kael-Vossen
- Appearance: Silver-white hair, imperial emerald clothing, dragon-skull crown
- Personality: Severe, intelligent, suspicious, politically experienced
- Notes: Her people carry generations of hatred toward House Valerius. She initially treats Abel and his companions as dangerous intruders but becomes interested in Abel's unusual Resonance.
