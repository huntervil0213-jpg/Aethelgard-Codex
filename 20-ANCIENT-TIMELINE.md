# 20 — THE DEEP TIMELINE

This is the long-range historical spine of Aethelgard. Dates are measured from the first surviving astronomical records. Earlier eras are deliberately described as deep prehistory because no modern calendar can accurately measure them.

## THE DEEP AGES

### Before the First Reckoning — The Uncounted Eons
The world existed long before mortal calendars. Primordial forces shaped oceans, mountains, forests, elemental regions, and the first magical currents. Dragons, giants, fey, elementals, and other ancient beings occupied the world before recognizable mortal kingdoms existed.

### The Age of First Blood
Early mortal peoples emerged and adapted to wildly different environments. There were no unified races in the modern political sense. Migration, magical adaptation, planar influence, and intermarriage produced many ancestral branches.

### The Age of Scale
Ancient dragons became dominant powers in several regions. Dragon lineages diverged dramatically. Some entered covenants with mortal populations, eventually contributing to the emergence of Dragonborn lineages. Other dragons rejected mortal integration and remained true dragons.

### The Age of Stone
Giant and dwarven civilizations expanded beneath and across the mountains. Early metallurgy, rune craft, masonry, and elemental magic developed together.

### The Age of Green Memory
Elven civilizations expanded through forests and ley-rich regions. Early canopy societies developed living architecture and preservation magic. Several elven languages diverged from their ancestral forms.

### The Age of Many Roads
Humans, halflings, gnomes, orcs, goblinoids, feline peoples, and numerous other cultures established independent civilizations. Trade connected peoples who had previously known one another only through myth.

### The First Concord
Ancient civilizations began deliberately exchanging magical and technological knowledge. The earliest multinational alliances formed. The period is remembered as an unusually productive era of scholarship.

### The Age of Arcane Architecture
Large magical structures appeared. Ley channels were mapped. Monoliths were constructed to regulate powerful currents. Early versions of modern Ley-Tech emerged.

### The Age of Extraction
Magical energy became an economic resource. Kingdoms learned to redirect ley currents on increasingly large scales. Extraction transformed from a local craft into industrial infrastructure.

### The Rise of the Spires
Power became concentrated in monumental urban centers. Political elites controlled magical technology, archives, trade routes, and access to education. The foundations of House Valerius power belong to this broader historical transformation.

### The Long Expansion
Industrial states expanded into frontier regions. Forests, wetlands, mineral basins, and ancient magical sites were rezoned or exploited. Resistance movements formed among displaced populations.

### The Filtration Era
Iron-Vein filtration networks became widespread. They reduced some forms of industrial contamination while enabling continued extraction. The technology therefore solved immediate problems while preserving the larger system that caused them.

### The Fracturing Years
Magical instability increased. Ley networks became increasingly unpredictable. Reality fissures appeared with greater frequency. Ancient safeguards that had been forgotten or dismantled became relevant again.

### The Present Era
The High-Reach remains a center of centralized power. Kael-Vossen preserves an alternative model of civilization based on ecological and magical balance. The outcasts' arrival connects these competing histories directly.

## Historical Principle

Aethelgard's present is the product of layers rather than a single ancient event. Civilizations rise, divide, merge, migrate, transform, and disappear. Surviving peoples carry fragments of much older worlds even when modern societies no longer remember where those traditions began.
