# AETHELGARD FAMILY TREES

This file is the deep family-history reference for the novel. The present-day relationships already established in the story are treated as canon. The older generations below expand those lines into Aethelgard’s ancient history.

## 1. HOUSE VALERIUS

### Present family

King Aldus Valerius
└── Cassian Valerius ── Lady Vivienne “Lady V” Valerius
    └── Abel Thorne Valerius

Malakor Valerius is Abel’s uncle and belongs to Cassian’s generation.

Lady Vivienne Valerius is Abel’s mother. She is not an unrelated Valerius noblewoman or a distant branch. Abel is the son of Cassian and Vivienne.

### Cassian’s branch

Cassian inherited the High-Reach throne from Aldus. His reign continued the vertical Spire system and its exploitation of the Ancestral Root and surrounding regions.

Abel is the direct child of Cassian and Vivienne, making him part of the central royal succession rather than a collateral Valerius claimant.

### Malakor’s branch

Malakor is Cassian’s brother. His exact political office may be expanded later, but his role in Abel’s childhood laboratories is established: he was directly involved in forcing Abel’s Resonance through Ley-Tech systems after the trait manifested at seven winters.

### Deep Valerius ancestry

The Valerius line is intentionally enormous. Its earliest recorded ancestors lived long before the modern High-Reach existed.

#### The Dawn-Crown Era — roughly 12,000+ years before the present

Valerion Veyr — remembered in later histories as the first recorded founder of the bloodline.

Maedra Veyr — keeper of the first family oath.

Orren Valerius — the first ancestor to consistently use the Valerius name in surviving records.

Selka Valerius — early treaty-maker between human river settlements.

#### The River Kingdom Era

The family spread through river cities, agricultural territories, mercenary companies, and early merchant houses. Several branches became judges, engineers, soldiers, and archivists.

The family did not begin as a royal dynasty. Its rise came through generations of marriages, military contracts, land ownership, and control of increasingly important infrastructure.

#### The First Ley Era

When organized study of magical currents accelerated, a Valerius branch became involved with early Ley conduits. This was the beginning of the family's eventual obsession with controlling energy rather than merely studying it.

#### The Vertical Crown Era

Later Valerius rulers began favoring vertical architecture, centralized authority, industrial extraction, and increasingly aggressive control of surrounding peoples.

This eventually produced the political philosophy inherited by Aldus and Cassian.

### Maternal line of Abel

Lady Vivienne’s ancestry is part of Abel’s canon family tree and is kept separate from the older patriarchal Valerius line until her own house history is expanded in greater detail.

Vivienne’s position within the royal household made her Abel’s mother and placed her directly inside the family conflict surrounding his upbringing, Resonance, and eventual banishment.

Her branch can carry its own history of marriages into House Valerius, court politics, and older human noble families without replacing Abel’s direct parentage.

## 2. VANE-SKAR FAMILY

Matron Vane-Skar
├── Jaxon
├── Vara
├── Theron
└── Kaelen Vane-Skar

The Vane-Skar family comes from the Zul-Thar marshlands. Their ancestry stretches through marsh clans, trackers, hunters, Silt Guard veterans, ferrymen, scouts, and territorial wardens.

### Ancient Vane-Skar history

Long before the Silt Guard existed, the earliest Vane-Skar ancestors were marsh pathfinders. Their survival depended on knowing which pools were safe, which reeds concealed predators, and which ground could swallow a heavily armored traveler.

The family eventually divided into several branches:

- Marsh-warden branch
- Hunter branch
- Silt Guard branch
- River-guide branch
- Scout branch
- Exile branch

The martial traditions inherited by Kaelen come from this long history rather than appearing suddenly in his generation.

## 3. EMERALD-WEFT FAMILY

Lyra Emerald-Weft belongs to a fallen canopy house whose prestige came from scholarship, diplomacy, language, treaty preservation, and historical archives.

Her uncle maintained seven regional lexicons and ancient boundary treaties. Lyra studied those materials during her childhood before the Inquisitors destroyed the estate.

### Ancient Emerald-Weft history

The earliest Emerald-Weft ancestors were canopy scribes who recorded oral histories on woven bark sheets before crystal archives became common.

Later branches became:

- Court linguists
- Treaty keepers
- Archive scholars
- Diplomatic interpreters
- Boundary historians
- Canopy judges

The family survived several political collapses by preserving information rather than territory.

## 4. KAEL-THAR FAMILY

Vaelin Kael-Thar’s immediate family remains less publicly documented, but the Kael-Thar name is associated with old elven border and shadow traditions.

Ancestors of the line served as scouts, intelligence gatherers, couriers, wardens, and covert defenders.

### Ancient branches

The oldest Kael-Thar records describe descendants living along the edge of early forest settlements before the Ley-Shield existed.

Branches later divided into:

- Border-watchers
- Shadow agents
- Forest scouts
- Courier houses
- Information keepers
- Exiled cells

The family's long relationship with secrecy explains why Vaelin instinctively studies patrol patterns, exits, weapons, and political danger.

## 5. IRON-MARROW FAMILY

Thrum Iron-Marrow belongs to a dwarven engineering and forge tradition.

The earliest Iron-Marrow ancestors were miners and stoneworkers who became known for building mechanisms intended to survive earthquakes, cave-ins, and centuries of use.

### Ancient branches

- Deep-forge smiths
- Siege engineers
- Ley-Tech mechanics
- Stone architects
- Weapon engineers
- Traveling repairers
- Experimental artificers

The family's oldest philosophy was simple: a machine that cannot survive its maker is unfinished.

Thrum's obsession with mechanisms, tools, joints, conduits, and structural design comes from this inherited craft culture.

## 6. SOL-VOSS FAMILY

Elara Sol-Voss belongs to a human religious lineage associated with Solis.

The oldest Sol-Voss ancestors were wandering shrine keepers who traveled between settlements before centralized religious authority existed.

### Ancient branches

- Shrine keepers
- Street priests
- Healers
- Pilgrims
- Religious scholars
- Dissidents
- Battlefield chaplains

The family accumulated a reputation for serving people outside wealthy districts. This helps explain why Elara’s faith is rooted in survival and direct service rather than palace ceremony.

## 7. KIKIASIE’S FELINE-KIN LINE

Kikiasie’s immediate family is not yet named in the novel. Her ancestral record therefore focuses on cultural branches rather than inventing a false immediate parentage.

Ancient Feline-Kin communities spread along trade roads, river settlements, caravan routes, scholarly cities, and forest margins.

Kiki’s potential ancestral branches include:

- Caravan traders
- Rooftop hunters
- Scholars
- Scouts
- River merchants
- Artisans
- Independent settlement clans

Feline-Kin family identity is often tied to community membership as much as blood descent. A clan can therefore preserve ancestry through adoption, oath-family, and migration records.

## 8. QUEEN ALTHAEA’S ROYAL LINE

Queen Althaea descends from the ancient ruling houses of Kael-Vossen.

Her royal ancestry reaches into the period when the basin was still part of the True Alliance and before the Ley-Shield isolated the civilization.

### Major eras

First Canopy Era — early forest settlements and root stewardship.

Rootkeeper Era — formal preservation of the Ancestral Root.

True Alliance Era — cooperation among elves, humans, halflings, tieflings, and other peoples.

War of Extraction — the period of escalating Valerius expansion.

Sealing Era — creation of the Ley-Shield.

Shield Dynasty — consolidation of Kael-Vossen's independent government.

Althaea’s Present Line — the current royal house.

## 9. DEEP-TIME ANCESTRAL HISTORY

The oldest family records in this Codex extend beyond ordinary written history.

### Before the kingdoms

More than ten thousand years before the present, organized racial kingdoms did not yet resemble their modern descendants. Elven canopy communities, dwarven deep settlements, human river tribes, halfling traveling clans, tiefling arcane enclaves, dragonborn lineages, and Feline-Kin communities existed as separate cultures with shifting territories.

### The First Alliance Age

The first major interspecies agreements established trade routes, shared magical research, and protection pacts. Several modern family names trace their earliest surviving traditions to these agreements.

### The Root Wars

Conflicts over magical resources fractured early alliances. Some families became guardians. Others became extractors. Several bloodlines disappeared completely.

### The First Spire Age

Vertical settlements began replacing older horizontal civilizations in regions controlled by expanding human powers. Families connected to engineering and Flux research gained enormous political influence.

### The Long Extraction

Over generations, the political systems surrounding Flux extraction hardened into institutions. House Valerius eventually became one of the strongest surviving powers within this system.

### The Fracture

Historical records from this era become increasingly contradictory. Entire peoples were renamed, erased, or classified as administrative errors. Some family branches survive only through oral traditions.

### The Present Age

The surviving descendants now carry thousands of years of cultural memory, even when they do not know the names of the people who came before them.

## 10. ANCESTRAL CONNECTIONS

Not every family in Aethelgard is secretly related.

Some bloodlines have never crossed.

Some intersected once through a marriage thousands of years ago and never reunited.

Some relationships exist through adoption, political oath, mentorship, or clan membership rather than blood.

The largest ancient connection between the major characters is cultural and historical: their ancestors lived through many of the same wars, migrations, resource conflicts, and magical transformations that shaped modern Aethelgard.

## 12. CANON STATUS

The immediate relationships already established in the novel are canon.

New ancestors and relatives created in this Codex are canon for the Codex and may be used by future chapters unless the novel later establishes a direct contradiction.

Lady Vivienne “Lady V” Valerius is canonically Abel Thorne Valerius’s mother.

Cassian Valerius is canonically Abel’s father.

King Aldus Valerius is canonically Abel’s grandfather.

Malakor is canonically Abel’s uncle.

Matron Vane-Skar is canonically Kaelen, Jaxon, Vara, and Theron’s mother.

The deeper branches are deliberately expansive so future chapters have a large historical foundation to draw from.
