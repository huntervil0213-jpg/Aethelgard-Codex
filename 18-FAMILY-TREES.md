# AETHELGARD FAMILY TREES

This is the family history section I use for the novel. The present-day relationships come from the story. The older branches below fill in the history around those characters and are now part of the working canon of the Codex.

The farther back a line goes, the more the records become fragmentary. That is intentional. Aethelgard has existed for ages, and no living kingdom possesses a complete record of every birth, death, marriage, exile, adoption, or broken bloodline.

# 1. HOUSE VALERIUS

## Current Generation

### King Cassian Valerius
Current ruler of the High-Reach and father of Abel Thorne Valerius.

Cassian inherited a political machine that was already centuries old. He did not create the entire system of extraction, vertical expansion, historical revision, and social control, but he became one of its strongest defenders.

### Lady Vivienne Valerius
An established member of House Valerius. Her exact immediate placement within the modern family is deliberately kept separate from assumptions about her parentage until the novel establishes it directly.

### Malakor Valerius
Established as Abel's uncle. He was involved in the alchemical experimentation performed on Abel after the manifestation of Abel's Resonance trait.

### Abel Thorne Valerius
Son of Cassian. Former prince of the High-Reach. Permanently banished after turning against the actions of his family and refusing to allow the Inquisitors to carry out their intended treatment of people close to him.

Abel is the first recent Valerius heir known to openly reject the political purpose of the family rather than simply oppose an individual ruler.

## Immediate Tree

**King Aldus Valerius**
└── **King Cassian Valerius**
    └── **Abel Thorne Valerius**

Malakor and Vivienne remain connected to the modern Valerius family, but their exact immediate branches are not overwritten here where the novel has not yet supplied the information.

## The First Valerius Era

Long before the High-Reach became the enormous vertical power it is in the present day, the Valerius name belonged to a comparatively minor human administrative family.

### Valerian Valerius, called the First Ledger-Keeper
Approximate age: more than 3,000 years before the present era.

Valerian was not originally a king. He was a records keeper attached to a human settlement that maintained trade agreements between several river communities. His importance came from organization rather than military power.

The earliest surviving Valerius fragments describe him as obsessive about measurements, inventories, boundary stones, taxation, and written agreements.

### Marcellan Valerius
Son of Valerian.

Marcellan transformed the family's reputation from clerical usefulness into political influence. He married into a merchant house and gained control of several regional transport agreements.

### Odran Valerius
Grandson of Marcellan.

Odran established the first Valerius military contracts. The family began providing engineers, quartermasters, surveyors, and administrators to expanding human settlements.

This period matters because the Valerius family did not begin as conquerors. Their later empire grew from control of information, infrastructure, and supply.

## The Era of the Vertical Cities

Approximately 2,500–1,900 years before the present.

During this era, several human houses began experimenting with increasingly vertical architecture and concentrated magical infrastructure. The Valerius family became closely associated with these projects.

### Seran Valerius
Created the first known Valerius-supported vertical civic district.

### Maevra Valerius
Established a family school for engineers, architects, and political administrators.

### Corvin Valerius
Expanded the family's influence over Flux transport routes.

### Teren Valerius
Negotiated early agreements with other human houses while secretly accumulating control over energy distribution.

The family doctrine gradually changed from “manage resources” to “control the systems that distribute resources.”

## The Root Expansion Period

Approximately 1,800–1,100 years before the present.

Valerius surveyors began studying the Ancestral Root and the enormous network of magical currents beneath Aethelgard.

This was the period in which the family first became interested in turning natural magical circulation into a centralized economic system.

### Avar Valerius
A military engineer who proposed linking regional Flux wells to central infrastructure.

### Selene Valerius
A political strategist who pushed the family toward permanent control of the routes connecting the wells.

### Dorian Valerius
An archivist whose work helped standardize the family's maps and legal records.

His surviving methods later became part of the bureaucratic traditions used by the Spire.

## The First Valerius Crown

Approximately 1,000 years before the present.

A Valerius ruler finally acquired enough influence to claim a crown.

The family stopped thinking of itself as one noble house among many and began presenting itself as the natural administrator of the continent.

This was the beginning of the ideology that later allowed the family to justify rewriting records belonging to other civilizations.

## The Age of Aldus

King Aldus Valerius became one of the most consequential rulers in the family tree.

According to the history Althaea later gives Abel, Aldus discovered that control of the physical intake valves of the Ancestral Root could determine the economic future of the continent.

His conquest was not initially based on armies.

It began through:

- trade restrictions
- altered historical records
- legal claims
- zoning decrees
- infrastructure control
- energy contracts
- administrative dependency
- political pressure

Aldus eventually escalated into direct military occupation.

His actions helped create the political conditions that led to the isolation of Kael-Vossen and the scattering of halfling and tiefling populations.

## Cassian's Branch

Cassian inherited Aldus's system rather than dismantling it.

His reign intensified the separation between the High-Reach and the populations living beneath it.

Abel's existence became a contradiction within that system. He possessed a natural Resonance trait that the family wanted to exploit, yet he eventually rejected the family ideology.

## Abel's Bloodline Significance

The Resonance trait manifested in Abel at seven winters old.

The family interpreted the manifestation as an opportunity for experimentation.

Malakor placed Abel into alchemical laboratories where his energy was forced through Ley-Tech conduits.

The experiments attempted to determine how much magical energy a human body could absorb before failure.

Abel's natural ability to project magic without an artificial focus distinguishes his bloodline from the majority of his family.

This does not mean every ancient Valerius possessed the same ability. The trait is unusual and should remain unusual.

# 2. VANE-SKAR FAMILY

## Current Generation

### Matron Vane-Skar
Mother of Kaelen, Jaxon, Vara, and Theron.

### Her Children

- Kaelen Vane-Skar
- Jaxon Vane-Skar
- Vara Vane-Skar
- Theron Vane-Skar

The family originates in the Zul-Thar marshlands and has a long connection to tracking, survival, territorial defense, and the Silt Guard.

## The First Marsh Ancestors

The oldest Vane-Skar traditions claim descent from a marsh-watcher who learned to navigate Zul-Thar before reliable maps existed.

### Vasker of the Black Reed
More than 2,000 years before the present.

Vasker was remembered as a hunter who could cross flooded reedlands without leaving an obvious trail.

The “Vane” portion of the modern name eventually became associated with wind, movement, and the reading of weather over open marshland.

The “Skar” portion came from an older clan word associated with a marked boundary, cut stone, or defended territory.

The combined name eventually became Vane-Skar.

## The Reed-Watcher Generations

Over many centuries the family produced:

- marsh hunters
- ferrymen
- scouts
- beast trackers
- herbalists
- water-route guides
- Silt Guard officers
- clan defenders

The family developed a reputation for noticing changes before other people did.

A broken reed.

A displaced stone.

A missing birdcall.

A change in water smell.

A footprint that should not exist.

These became part of Vane-Skar survival culture.

## The Silt Guard Branch

Approximately 700 years before the present, several Vane-Skar descendants formally joined the Silt Guard.

The family became increasingly associated with protecting settlements from monsters, raiders, poisonous marsh conditions, and hostile territory.

This tradition eventually reached Matron Vane-Skar and her children.

## Kaelen's Generation

Kaelen inherited the physical toughness and territorial instincts of the family, but his relationship with the Silt Guard is shaped by the suffering inflicted upon his people.

His family history should not be reduced to warriors. The Vane-Skar line contains healers, trackers, cooks, boat builders, hunters, and ordinary marsh families alongside soldiers.

# 3. EMERALD-WEFT FAMILY

## Current Generation

### Lyra Emerald-Weft
Member of a fallen canopy house.

Her uncle's private archives contained seven regional lexicons and ancient boundary treaties. Lyra spent winters translating those records before the Inquisitors burned her family's estate.

Her ability to understand the old high-court elven dialect comes from study rather than years of conversation. She is therefore knowledgeable but rusty when speaking it aloud.

This distinction is important.

Lyra can recognize grammar, titles, treaty forms, ceremonial expressions, and old vocabulary more reliably than she can produce spontaneous conversation.

## Ancient Origins

The Emerald-Weft family began as a scholarly canopy line rather than a royal dynasty.

### Aelirra Emerald-Weft
Approximately 2,700 years before the present.

Aelirra preserved oral histories by weaving colored threads through ceremonial cloth. Different patterns represented family events, treaties, deaths, migrations, and political changes.

The technique eventually gave the family its name.

## The Archive Keepers

For more than a millennium, Emerald-Weft descendants became librarians, linguists, treaty keepers, diplomats, historians, and translators.

They maintained records from:

- canopy houses
- human kingdoms
- halfling settlements
- tiefling communities
- dwarven trade clans
- Feline-Kin caravans
- old border peoples

Their archive was valuable precisely because it crossed cultural boundaries.

## The Seven Lexicons

The private collection inherited by Lyra's uncle was built over generations.

Each regional lexicon represented a different period or political region.

Some contained extinct dialects.

Some contained ceremonial forms no longer used in ordinary conversation.

Some contained words whose meaning had changed over centuries.

This is why Lyra's knowledge of old Elven can sound formal, archaic, and occasionally hesitant.

## The Fall of the House

The Inquisitors burned the Emerald-Weft estate.

Most of the family's modern records were destroyed.

Lyra survived with only fragments of the knowledge her family preserved.

Her survival is therefore part of the family's historical continuity.

# 4. KAEL-THAR FAMILY

## Current Generation

### Vaelin Kael-Thar
A shadow rogue whose ancestry reaches into old elven border and covert-defense traditions.

His family history is deliberately less public than the Emerald-Weft records.

## The First Kael-Thar

### Kaelith Thar
More than 2,500 years before the present.

Kaelith belonged to an early border community that specialized in moving through forest territory without disturbing it.

The name Kael-Thar developed from two older clan identifiers that eventually merged after a series of marriages between neighboring houses.

## The Night-Warden Generations

The Kael-Thar line produced:

- scouts
- messengers
- wardens
- intelligence keepers
- hunters
- infiltrators
- border guides
- covert defenders

Unlike the Emerald-Wefts, they rarely preserved history in grand archives.

Their records were hidden in coded marks, route maps, knots, scratched symbols, and oral traditions.

## The Shadow Faction

Several generations of Kael-Thar descendants became associated with the shadow faction that eventually suffered under Valerius expansion.

The destruction of border territories left the family with an inherited hatred of the High-Reach.

Vaelin's restraint does not erase that history.

It makes his continued cooperation with Abel more significant.

# 5. IRON-MARROW FAMILY

## Current Generation

### Thrum Iron-Marrow
Dwarf engineer and member of the outcast group.

His immediate parents are not established in the novel, so this Codex establishes them now.

### Garrik Iron-Marrow
Thrums father. Traveling forge engineer.

### Brunna Iron-Marrow
Thrums mother. Stoneworks mechanic and structural inspector.

### Their children

- Thrum Iron-Marrow
- Dorrin Iron-Marrow, older brother, deceased
- Hessa Iron-Marrow, younger sister

## The Deep Forge Ancestors

### Morgran Iron-Marrow
More than 3,000 years before the present.

Morgran was a deep-forge smith who developed a reputation for producing tools that could survive extreme heat and magical pressure.

The name Iron-Marrow became associated with the belief that a craftsperson should be as dependable as the metal inside a well-made tool.

## The Forge Generations

Across thousands of years, Iron-Marrow descendants became:

- blacksmiths
- armorers
- bridge builders
- miners
- siege engineers
- magical engineers
- stonecutters
- Ley-Tech repairers
- traveling mechanics

The family is not exclusively tied to warfare.

Their most respected members often repaired infrastructure that kept entire communities alive.

## The Ley-Tech Split

Several centuries before the present, one Iron-Marrow branch accepted contracts from the High-Reach.

Another branch refused.

The disagreement split the family politically.

The High-Reach branch gained money and prestige.

The independent branch preserved older dwarven engineering traditions.

Thrum descends from the independent tradition.

This gives his fascination with unfamiliar machinery a family history rather than making it a random personality trait.

# 6. SOL-VOSS FAMILY

## Current Generation

### Elara Sol-Voss
Priestess of Solis and member of the outcast group.

Her family has a long religious tradition, but the Sol-Voss name is not synonymous with wealth or institutional power.

## The First Sol-Voss

### Solan Voss
Approximately 2,200 years before the present.

Solan was a traveling shrine keeper who maintained roadside sanctuaries for workers, travelers, refugees, and people who could not afford formal temple services.

The family eventually adopted Sol-Voss as a permanent name.

## The Street-Shrine Tradition

The Sol-Voss family produced generations of:

- priests
- shrine keepers
- healers
- street missionaries
- undertakers
- traveling clerics
- sanctuary wardens
- religious dissidents

They believed faith belonged to ordinary people as much as kings.

This tradition eventually shaped Elara's street-hardened worldview.

## The High-Reach Religious Split

Several generations ago, one Sol-Voss branch accepted a position within the High-Reach religious hierarchy.

Another branch remained independent.

Elara's branch descended from the independent tradition.

That split explains why the family can carry strong devotion to Solis without automatically supporting the political institutions surrounding the High-Reach.

# 7. KIKIASIE'S FELINE-KIN FAMILY

## Current Generation

### Kikiasie
Usually called Kiki.

Kiki descends from a Feline-Kin family whose ancestors moved along old trade roads connecting settlements separated by hundreds of miles.

Unlike human houses, the family's identity is not based on a single permanent estate.

## The First Caravan Ancestors

### Kiasa-of-the-Silver-Ear
More than 2,000 years before the present.

Kiasa was a caravan guide remembered for navigating trade routes using stars, scent, and terrain.

The name Kikiasie is a later descendant form of an older family naming tradition.

## The Traveling Branches

Kiki's ancestors include:

- caravan traders
- scouts
- scholars
- hunters
- performers
- merchants
- locksmiths
- couriers
- wandering healers

The family maintained connections across several Feline-Kin communities rather than remaining concentrated in one city.

## The Scholar Branch

One ancient branch became known for preserving information through portable crystal records.

That tradition explains why Kiki is comfortable around valuable documents and why she understands that information can be worth more than coin.

## The Caravan Branch

Another branch remained focused on trade and travel.

They developed extensive knowledge of roads, taverns, merchants, border guards, and local customs.

Kiki inherits pieces of both traditions.

# 8. QUEEN ALTHAEA'S ROYAL LINE

## Current Queen

### Queen Althaea
Ruler of Kael-Vossen.

Her dynasty predates the modern Ley-Shield and reaches back to the ancient alliance that once connected the basin with other civilizations.

## The First Rootkeeper Dynasty

### Aelthara Root-Bearer
More than 4,000 years before the present.

Aelthara is remembered as one of the earliest rulers to formalize stewardship of the Ancestral Root.

She did not own the Root.

The earliest canopy philosophy held that no ruler could own a living source of the world's energy.

A ruler could only protect it.

## The Rootkeeper Generations

The dynasty produced:

- wardens
- diplomats
- agricultural scholars
- healers
- Ley-current researchers
- historians
- military defenders
- environmental stewards

The monarchy therefore developed differently from the High-Reach throne.

Its legitimacy came from preservation and stewardship rather than extraction.

## The Alliance Era

Approximately 1,500–1,000 years before the present, the royal line helped maintain the True Alliance.

Humans, elves, halflings, tieflings, dwarves, and other peoples traded knowledge and resources through interconnected settlements.

The alliance was imperfect.

There were disputes, rivalries, prejudice, territorial conflicts, and political disagreements.

It was never a perfect golden age.

But the system allowed civilizations to exchange knowledge without placing one empire above all others.

## The Valerius Conflict

King Aldus Valerius changed that balance.

His administration targeted the systems supporting the alliance.

The conflict eventually became violent.

The elves were pushed toward the basin.

The Ley-Shield was established.

The borders closed.

The royal line survived.

The old alliance did not.

## Althaea's Inheritance

Althaea inherited not only the crown but the memory of the destruction of the alliance.

This explains her hatred of House Valerius.

It also explains why Abel's appearance creates a contradiction for her.

He carries the bloodline responsible for the suffering of her people while actively rejecting the system that produced it.

She does not immediately forgive him.

She does not forget history.

She studies him because he may be the first opportunity in generations to determine whether the Valerius bloodline can produce something other than another ruler.

# 9. THE ANCIENT CANOPY LINES

The modern elven houses descend from much older communities.

Thousands upon thousands of years ago, elves lived in scattered forest civilizations rather than a single unified kingdom.

The oldest family records refer to three broad ancestral traditions.

## The Root-Bearers

Early protectors of ancient magical forests.

Their descendants became:

- druids
- healers
- gardeners
- root scholars
- wardens

## The Star-Scribes

Early observers and record keepers who studied celestial movements and magical currents.

Their descendants contributed to:

- astronomy
- magical cartography
- navigation
- calendar systems
- Ley-current prediction

## The Canopy Houses

Later political families formed from the merging of older forest clans.

The Emerald-Weft and several other houses descend from this period.

The Canopy Houses were never one perfectly united family.

They competed over trade, scholarship, military authority, and access to ancient knowledge.

Their eventual alliance was built through necessity as much as friendship.

# 10. CROSS-HOUSE CONNECTIONS

The major families of the present story are not completely isolated from one another.

However, most connections are ancient and indirect.

## Valerius and Emerald-Weft

A Valerius diplomatic branch once employed Emerald-Weft translators during an early treaty period.

The relationship ended when Valerius political expansion turned against the canopy houses.

Some Emerald-Weft records were preserved precisely because translators secretly copied documents before the political purge.

## Valerius and Kael-Thar

Valerius expansion displaced several Kael-Thar-associated border communities.

The relationship is therefore hostile rather than familial in the modern era.

## Valerius and Iron-Marrow

Some dwarven engineers worked on early vertical infrastructure.

The Iron-Marrow family later divided over whether cooperation with the High-Reach was acceptable.

## Sol-Voss and Feline-Kin caravans

Sol-Voss shrines were common stopping places along old caravan roads.

Kiki's distant ancestors would have encountered Sol-Voss priests repeatedly during their migrations.

## Emerald-Weft and Kael-Thar

The two elven lines occasionally worked together.

Emerald-Weft scholars needed safe routes between archives.

Kael-Thar scouts knew those routes.

This relationship predates the current characters by centuries.

## Althaea's Line and Emerald-Weft

The Emerald-Wefts were historically connected to the royal archive system.

Lyra's family therefore carries an old cultural connection to Althaea's court even though Lyra herself enters the kingdom as an outsider.

# 11. FAMILY SECRETS AND LOST BRANCHES

## The Lost Valerius Branch

A Valerius branch disappeared approximately 600 years ago after refusing to support centralized Flux extraction.

Some records suggest the descendants changed their names and disappeared into ordinary human settlements.

Their modern identity is unknown.

## The Broken Iron-Marrow Branch

A dwarven branch accepted permanent High-Reach contracts and eventually abandoned the Iron-Marrow name.

Their descendants may still exist among the great engineering guilds.

## The Burned Emerald-Weft Records

Not every Emerald-Weft archive was destroyed.

Several hidden records were removed before the estate burned.

Some may still exist in forgotten canopy vaults.

## The Forgotten Sol-Voss Line

An ancient Sol-Voss branch abandoned organized religion and became wandering healers.

Their descendants may have retained fragments of older Solis traditions no longer practiced by modern temples.

## The Unnamed Kael-Thar Branch

One Kael-Thar branch vanished during the early Valerius border wars.

No confirmed descendants are currently known.

## The Old Feline-Kin Caravan Line

Kiki's family has distant relatives scattered across multiple trade routes.

Many use different surnames or single names, making the full genealogy almost impossible to reconstruct.

# 13. ANCESTRAL NAMING NOTES

Aethelgard does not use one universal naming system.

Human noble houses usually preserve surnames through generations.

Dwarven families often attach identity to craft, clan, forge, or ancestral reputation.

Elven houses can preserve names for centuries, but old names may change pronunciation as dialects evolve.

Feline-Kin families often maintain personal names, caravan names, descriptive names, or community identifiers that may change during migration.

Religious families such as Sol-Voss can preserve names through spiritual traditions rather than noble inheritance.

This is why an ancient ancestor does not always have a name that looks identical to the modern family name.

# CANON NOTES

The family histories above are now part of the Aethelgard Codex's canon unless a later novel chapter deliberately changes them.

Ancient relatives are not automatically destined to appear in the main story. Their purpose is to make the modern characters feel like descendants of civilizations that existed long before the current conflict.

The deeper a genealogy reaches into the past, the more likely the records become incomplete, contradictory, ceremonial, or lost. That gives the world room for future discoveries without requiring every ancient event to be known by modern characters.

The important point is that the people traveling together now are not isolated inventions. Each carries thousands of years of family history behind them, even when they personally know only a fraction of it.
