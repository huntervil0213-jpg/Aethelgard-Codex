# AETHELGARD FAMILY TREES

This is the family and bloodline reference for the story. Known relationships come first. Older generations are working branches created to give the houses history; they can be changed later if the novel establishes something different.

## 1. HOUSE VALERIUS

### Known line

**King Aldus Valerius**
└── **Cassian Valerius**
    └── **Abel Thorne Valerius**

### Known connections
- Aldus Valerius — grandfather of Abel; former king and architect of the older Valerius expansion.
- Cassian Valerius — father of Abel; current king of the High-Reach.
- Abel Thorne Valerius — former prince, now permanently banished.
- Lady Vivienne Valerius — established member of the Valerius family; exact placement in the immediate tree is not yet fixed by the novel.
- Malakor — established as Abel's uncle; exact parentage branch remains open until the story confirms whether he is Cassian's brother or belongs to another Valerius branch.

### Proposed ancient Valerius line

The Valerius dynasty can be traced through many generations of human rulers, nobles, military houses, archivists, and early Flux authorities. The oldest names below are working names, not locked canon.

**Era of the First Crown**
- Valerian the First — founder of the early Valerius house.
- Marcellan Valerius — second-generation ruler and organizer of the first crown bureaucracy.
- Odran Valerius — expanded the family's influence through trade and military contracts.

**Era of Expansion**
- Several Valerius branches formed from younger children marrying into merchant, military, and scholarly families.
- One branch eventually became connected to the early Spire institutions.
- Another branch disappeared after a succession conflict.

**Era of the Spire**
- The family increasingly tied its legitimacy to Flux extraction, Ley-Tech, and vertical architecture.
- Aldus became one of the most historically consequential Valerius rulers.
- Cassian inherited that political system.
- Abel became the first known recent heir to openly reject the family's direction.

## 2. HOUSE VANE-SKAR

### Known family

**Matron Vane-Skar**
├── **Kaelen Vane-Skar**
├── **Jaxon**
├── **Vara**
└── **Theron**

The siblings and their mother are established. Their father's identity and older ancestry have not yet been established in the novel.

### Working ancestral branch

The Vane-Skar line can be treated as an old martial bloodline originating in the Zul-Thar marshlands.

Earlier generations were marsh guardians, trackers, Silt Guard veterans, hunters, and clan leaders. The family's survival depended on reading water, mud, scent, movement, and territorial changes.

Older Vane-Skar branches may have split into:
- Silt Guard branch
- Marsh hunter branch
- Scout branch
- Clan elder branch
- Exiled branch

The exact ancestral names remain open for later canon.

## 3. EMERALD-WEFT LINE

### Known line

**Lyra Emerald-Weft**

Lyra is a member of a fallen canopy house. Her family maintained private archives containing regional lexicons and ancient boundary treaties.

Her parents and grandparents have not been named in the novel yet. The family tree therefore remains intentionally open.

### Working history

The Emerald-Weft house is an old scholarly canopy lineage whose influence came from language, archives, diplomacy, treaty preservation, and historical memory rather than military conquest.

The house's oldest ancestors may reach back to the first canopy settlements of Kael-Vossen.

Potential branches:
- Archive branch
- Treaty branch
- Linguistic branch
- Canopy court branch

## 4. KAEL-THAR LINE

### Known line

**Vaelin Kael-Thar**

Vaelin's immediate family has not yet been established in the novel. His ancestry should therefore remain expandable rather than pretending unknown parents are canon.

### Working ancient branch

The Kael-Thar name can belong to an old elven shadow and border lineage. Earlier members may have served as scouts, information keepers, wardens, couriers, and covert defenders of settlements.

The oldest Kael-Thar records could predate the sealing of Kael-Vossen by many centuries.

## 5. IRON-MARROW LINE

### Known line

**Thrum Iron-Marrow**

Thrum's immediate family is currently unknown.

### Working ancestry

Iron-Marrow can be developed as an old dwarven forge lineage. The family name suggests a cultural tradition centered on endurance, metallurgy, engineering, and the belief that good craft should outlive its maker.

Potential ancient branches:
- Deep-forge branch
- Ley-Tech engineering branch
- Weaponsmith branch
- Stoneworks branch
- Traveling engineer branch

## 6. SOL-VOSS LINE

### Known line

**Elara Sol-Voss**

Elara's immediate family has not yet been fully established.

Her name connects her identity strongly to Solis and her priesthood, but the exact relationship between the Sol-Voss family and the religious institution remains open.

### Working ancestry

The Sol-Voss line can reach back through generations of human priests, street missionaries, healers, shrine keepers, and religious dissidents.

Older branches may have served different regions before the High-Reach centralized religious authority.

## 8. QUEEN ALTHAEA'S ROYAL LINE

### Known

**Queen Althaea** — Queen of Kael-Vossen.

Her parents and earlier ancestors have not yet been named.

### Working royal ancestry

Althaea's dynasty can descend from the ancient rulers who governed the basin before the borders were sealed.

The oldest royal line reaches back to the formation of the canopy alliance and the preservation of the Ancestral Root.

Possible succession branches include:
- First Canopy dynasty
- Rootkeeper dynasty
- Alliance dynasty
- Shield dynasty
- Present royal line

These names are placeholders for future development.

## 9. HOW ANCIENT THE TREES GO

For major houses, the family records can eventually be organized into:

1. Present generation
2. Parents
3. Grandparents
4. Great-grandparents
5. Founding generations
6. Early kingdoms
7. Pre-Spire civilizations
8. First recorded alliances
9. Ancient migrations
10. Mythic ancestry
11. Prehistoric racial origins

The oldest layers should be used carefully. A character does not automatically inherit every trait, ability, or cultural identity of an ancient ancestor.

## 10. FAMILY TREE RULES

- Established relationships from the novel always take priority.
- Unknown relatives stay marked as unknown until needed.
- New ancestors can be created when the story needs depth.
- A created ancestor does not automatically become a future plot fact.
- Marriage alliances should have political consequences where appropriate.
- Branches can die out.
- Houses can split, merge, change names, or lose titles.
- Adoption, illegitimacy, exile, and hidden branches can exist where the story supports them.
- Bloodline magic should remain rare and specific rather than appearing in every generation.
- Ancient ancestry should add history without making every modern character secretly connected.
