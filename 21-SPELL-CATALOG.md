# 21 — SPELL CATALOG & ARCANE NAMING

## Ancient Spell Names

Spell names should reflect the language and culture that created them. A spell can have an ancient name, a scholarly translation, and a modern battlefield name.

### Frost and Resonance

**Veyrith Auralen** — an ancient frost-resonance working that draws heat from an area and crystallizes unstable magical currents.

**Khaur Velesh** — a defensive frost pulse designed to interrupt another spell before it completes.

**Ssaelith Norem** — a narrow lance of condensed cold used for precision strikes.

### Root and Living Magic

**Elyssara Thuun** — encourages damaged roots to reconnect and stabilize a wounded magical grove.

**Moraeth Velyn** — temporarily shares sensory information between a caster and a living tree network.

### Ley-Tech

**Qoraveth Iluun** — a stabilization sequence used in large magical arrays.

**Thal'keryn Vos** — redirects excess magical current into a prepared secondary channel.

### Shadow

**Nhaur Kessai** — suppresses the caster's visible magical signature for a short period.

**Velqira Ashuun** — bends available shadow around the caster to obscure movement.

### Radiance

**Aureth Valora** — creates a concentrated field of cleansing light.

**Seyrath Orun** — reveals magical traces hidden beneath ordinary sight.

### Void / Reality

**Qethra Nul** — an extremely dangerous attempt to sever a localized magical connection to surrounding reality.

**Ossivar Keth** — a containment working designed to prevent a fissure from widening.

## Spell-Casting Notes

Ancient spell names are not interchangeable passwords. Their sounds, rhythm, gestures, and mental structures vary by culture.

A scholar may know a spell's translated meaning without being capable of casting it. Conversely, a practiced caster may perform a spell correctly without understanding the ancient language that originally named it.

## D&D Compatibility

The catalog is designed to fit alongside familiar D&D concepts such as cantrips, leveled spells, rituals, concentration, components, spellcasting foci, spell slots, innate spellcasting, legendary magic, and class-based spell lists.

The Aethelgard-specific terminology adds history and culture without requiring every existing D&D spell to be renamed.
