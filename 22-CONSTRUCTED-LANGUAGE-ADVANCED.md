# 22 — ADVANCED CONSTRUCTED LANGUAGE

## What this is

This is the working linguistic foundation for the major constructed languages used in Aethelgard. The goal is for the languages to feel old, regional, and internally consistent without making every invented word look like a variation of the same word.

## High Elven

High Elven is a formal language with several registers. Court speech is deliberately precise. Border speech is shorter and more practical. Ritual speech preserves archaic forms that ordinary citizens may not understand.

### Sound rules

High Elven avoids ordinary Common-language vocabulary. Its romanization favors unusual consonant clusters, long vowels, apostrophes used sparingly, and syllable patterns that remain distinct from English.

### Core roots

- **aevr** — sovereign authority
- **qel** — boundary
- **thae** — oath
- **vyrn** — bloodline
- **shael** — stranger
- **oruq** — judgment
- **nhaev** — exile
- **lyss** — living grove
- **kaor** — shield
- **zevra** — witness

These roots should not be mechanically repeated in every compound. Natural languages develop irregular forms, contractions, inherited synonyms, and regional alternatives.

### Registers

**Court:** longer constructions, explicit titles, careful grammar.

**Military:** clipped phrases, commands, positional terminology.

**Ritual:** archaic vocabulary and repeated ceremonial structures.

**Casual:** contractions and shortened forms.

**Hostile:** direct constructions, omitted honorifics, and ancestral insults.

### Lyra's fluency

Lyra learned High Elven from archives and treaties. She can recognize formal vocabulary and reconstruct written grammar extremely well, but spontaneous conversation is harder. She may pause, substitute an archaic word, use the wrong register, or mentally translate before answering.

She should sound educated but rusty—not incompetent and not perfectly native.

## Ancient Draconic

Ancient Draconic predates modern Dragonborn dialects. It is built around breath, rhythm, force, and distinctions between elemental and social authority.

Its modern descendants include multiple draconic dialect families. Dragonborn languages are therefore related to Ancient Draconic without being identical to it.

## Dwarven Tongues

Dwarven languages developed from subterranean clan dialects. Craft vocabulary is unusually precise because a single word may distinguish material quality, forging temperature, structural stress, or intended use.

## Feline-Kin Speech

Feline-Kin languages favor rhythm, emphasis, and context. Different communities may use different levels of directness. A playful phrase can become insulting if spoken with the wrong cadence.

## Translator Compatibility

The story's fictional languages should be recorded as stable romanized text rather than relying on fonts or symbols that external translators cannot process.

When a D&D language translator is used, the Codex entry should identify the intended D&D language or closest equivalent. The fictional vocabulary itself remains Aethelgard canon; the translator is a tool for pronunciation or presentation, not an authority over the novel's meanings.

## Language Growth Rule

Do not generate a new word merely by changing one letter of an existing word. New vocabulary should have visibly different shapes, sounds, and histories. Repeated roots should occur only when they have an actual grammatical, cultural, or historical reason.
